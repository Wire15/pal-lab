# Pal Lab 1.9.0

**Captured humans are first-class.** If you've sphere'd a wandering merchant, a
syndicate thug, or a bounty target, Pal Lab now knows who they are.

## Added

- **Human detail card.** Clicking a captured human opens a proper card —
  localized name, faction (Rayne Syndicate, PIDF, Free Pal Alliance, Wandering
  Merchant, Villager...), level, HP/ATK/DEF, and base work suitabilities —
  instead of an "unknown pal" error. Covers all 401 human NPCs.
- **Human portraits.** Real archetype art in the palbox, roster, and card,
  including unique portraits for all 34 bounty targets (Hawk, Grill, Ego...).
  Bounty humans keep the Alpha badge, matching the game.
- **Roster polish.** Display names instead of raw ids (`Male_People03` →
  "Villager"), em-dashes instead of fake 0 IVs, palbox search finds humans by
  name, and the header counts split into "N pals · M humans".

Humans remain correctly excluded from the breeding solver — they can work, not
breed. Human data and portraits vendored from
[palworld-save-pal](https://github.com/oMaN-Rod/palworld-save-pal) (see
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES; portraits are Palworld game art © Pocketpair).

**Windows SmartScreen:** this is an unsigned build. If SmartScreen warns you,
click **More info -> Run anyway**.
