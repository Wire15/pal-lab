# Pal Lab 1.6.0

**Xbox / Game Pass saves now load directly — no conversion tool needed.**

## For Game Pass players

Until now you had to convert your save with a separate tool before Pal Lab
could read it. That step is gone:

- **Desktop:** hit the new **Xbox / Game Pass** button on the load screen.
  Pal Lab finds the Game Pass save store on your PC, shows your worlds
  (name, players, last played), and loads the one you pick. **Read-only —
  nothing is ever written to your save.**
- **Web:** drop the store folder onto [pal-lab.pages.dev](https://pal-lab.pages.dev):

  ```
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\PocketpairInc.Palworld_ad4psfrxyesvt\SystemAppData\wgs
  ```

  Parsed 100% client-side in WebAssembly, like every other save. Save
  remembrance ("Restore") works for Xbox worlds too.

Once loaded, an Xbox world is a first-class citizen: breeding solver, IV Lab,
Pal-dex owned filters, share links — everything works, and the app remembers
the world across restarts.

The chunked **CNK** save compression Xbox uses is decoded natively on both
platforms.

## Honest notes

- World Map fog-of-war isn't available for Xbox saves yet.
- Xbox **console** saves need to sync to the PC Game Pass app once (just open
  the world on PC) before they exist on your machine.
- Live save-watching is off for Xbox saves — the store has no stable folder
  to watch. Reopen the world from the load screen to re-read it.

Format implemented from the layouts documented by
[palworld-save-pal](https://github.com/oMaN-Rod/palworld-save-pal),
[palworld-save-tools](https://github.com/cheahjs/palworld-save-tools), and
[XGP-save-extractor](https://github.com/Z1ni/XGP-save-extractor) (all MIT;
nothing vendored — see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES).
